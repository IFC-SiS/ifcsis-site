India Has Written Four of the Five Laws

The parent owns the line. The child uses it. The reservoir makes it safe.

21 October 2026 · S. Harrison Knudson, J.D.

India has already settled the legal questions other countries are still arguing: a child cannot contract, both parents are guardians, and a platform needs a parent's consent. What no one has yet built is the infrastructure that lets a child use a connection the parent owns.

The five pieces

RuleOwns / usesStatus
No connection for minors.Parent ownsIn force (as reported)

A verified adult behind every connection.Parent ownsIn force since December 2023; real-time checks due 30 November 2026
The child's connection, drawing only from the reservoir.Child usesNot yet built — the standard IFC-SiS writes

Operators may not sell a SIM to anyone under 18, because the customer form is a contract and a minor cannot contract. Platforms may not let anyone under 18 open a social media account on their own. Verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian is required before processing a child's data. Digital KYC applies to every new connection, with a limit per adult. The fifth piece is the one that does not exist: the parent's act, rather than a platform's guess, identifying the child.

The reservoir

A city does not ban rivers. It builds a tested supply and pipes it to the home. The reservoir works the same way: it begins empty and admits only what meets a published standard. Nothing is removed from the open internet. It is not a ban, and it collects no new ID. It gives the laws above something to deliver, and gives parents the power the law already assigns them.

The standard: one format, filled in by each country

Every descriptor is a code, not a word. Each has a stable, language-neutral code with a label in every language, so the same code means the same thing everywhere.

Accurate or additional. A translation must keep a descriptor's meaning exactly. A country may add its own descriptors, but never redefine, remove or weaken a core one.

Descriptive, not evaluative. Observable facts translate; judgments such as “inappropriate” do not. A progressive parent and a conservative parent can use the same labels to make opposite choices.

Like a date. 2026-10-21 reads the same on every computer. IFC-SiS owns the format; each nation owns what goes in it. Operators build to the standard.

For discussion

Would a sample of core descriptors translate accurately into a few Indian languages, and which additional descriptors would India need? The format is built for any language, and would be tested first in a few. And where do children, and the parent's enrollment, belong in an evidence base on AI safety in the Global South?

Scope and evidence

The SIM rule is reported in telecom trade press, with the departmental circular still to be confirmed. Other sources are the DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025, Department of Telecommunications KYC and connection-limit circulars of 2023 and 2026, and the Supreme Court of India hearing of 28 September 2026 as reported. Items marked as reported require a primary-source pin before they are relied upon.
