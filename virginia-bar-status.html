Steele Harrison Knudson: Virginia Bar Status and Background

S. Harrison Knudson, J.D. · Statement of November 27, 2026

Summary

Steele Harrison Knudson (professionally S. Harrison Knudson, J.D.) was seriously injured on May 15, 2023, when the pickup truck he was riding in as a back-seat passenger was struck from behind on eastbound I-66 in Fairfax County, Virginia. About a year of pain management, four surgeries (both hips and his spine) and physical therapy followed. While he was receiving that medical treatment, he consented, on the advice of counsel, to the Virginia State Bar suspending his Virginia law license on impairment grounds, effective July 12, 2024. The suspension was not imposed for misconduct. As of November 2026, his petition to terminate the suspension is pending.

Status

On July 12, 2024, the Virginia State Bar Disciplinary Board suspended my Virginia law license indefinitely on impairment grounds by consent order. On the advice of counsel, I consented to that suspension while I was receiving medical treatment for injuries from the crash described below. As of November 2026, my petition to terminate the suspension is pending. The Virginia State Bar’s notice and order are public.

Background

The medical history behind that period began on May 15, 2023, when I was riding as a back-seat passenger in a pickup truck stopped on the shoulder of eastbound I-66 in Fairfax County. A car ran off the road, struck the Virginia State Police vehicle stopped behind us, and drove it into the rear of the truck. I spent about a year in pain management, underwent four surgeries, including both hips and my spine, and continued my recovery through physical therapy. I’m sharing this background because the public notice records the Board’s action without describing the accident or the course of treatment.

News coverage of the crash: WTOP · WUSA9 · Patch
